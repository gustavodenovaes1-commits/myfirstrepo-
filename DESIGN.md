## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, craft, and convivial atmosphere of an artisanal Italian-American pizzeria and trattoria. It marries the timeless romance of old-world Neapolitan craftsmanship with clean, modern editorial restraint. The visual personality is tactile, welcoming, and culinary-forward—evoking sun-baked terracotta ovens, dusted semolina flour, slow-simmered San Marzano tomatoes, and aged linen tablecloths.

The target audience encompasses neighborhood food lovers, families gathering for celebratory dinners, and modern digital diners ordering curated takeaway. The interface must evoke tactile comfort and appetite appeal without resorting to kitsch. 

The aesthetic combines **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Nuances**:
- Generous, breathing margins reminiscent of printed heavy cardstock menus.
- Delicate rule lines, classic menu leaders, and structured typographic columns that prioritize scannability.
- Natural, organic warmth in place of cold digital neutrals.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural, culinary raw materials:

- **Primary (`#B93826` — San Marzano Tomato):** A rich, rustic terracotta red applied to primary interactive elements, brand accents, featured dishes, and callouts.
- **Secondary (`#5C6B47` — Tuscan Olive Leaf):** A muted, earthy green used for dietary indicators (vegetarian, vegan), fresh pairing notes, secondary tags, and subtle contextual highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B26` — Toasted Semolina Crust):** A deep, warm honeyed ochre used sparingly for specials, chef's recommendations, and star ratings.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1C1917` — Roasted Espresso):** Deep charcoal brown-black for core text, prices, and line art, providing soft yet commanding contrast against warm paper backgrounds.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAF7F2` (Linen Flour), avoiding harsh sterile whites.
  - Surface cards & overlays: `#FFFFFF` (Fresh Ricotta), used softly for elevated modals and order summaries.
  - Borders & Dividers: `#E7E2D8` (Stone Hearth), providing subtle low-contrast structure.

## Typography

The typographical pairing sets a deliberate tension between editorial heritage and functional clarity.

- **Headlines & Section Titles (Playfair Display):** Conveys the classic gravitas of trattoria broadsheets and vintage wine lists. Used strictly for section headers, category dividers, and dish titles to create elegance and dining atmosphere.
- **Body & Functional Specs (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A friendly, warm humanist sans-serif. Used for culinary descriptions (crushed San Marzano, fior di latte, 72-hour cold fermented dough), modifier lists, dietary specifications, and prices to ensure immediate legibility even under dim restaurant lighting.
- **Label & Badging:** Uses uppercase letterspacing (`0.08em`) with heavy font weights to anchor metadata such as multi-size indicators (`12" PERSONAL`, `16" SHARING`) and crust types.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows an editorial fixed-fluid hybrid structure modeled after physical restaurant menus:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column layout or dual-column editorial split (`50% / 50%`) designed for side-by-side menu categories (e.g., *Pizze Rosse* on the left, *Pizze Bianche* on the right). Section headers span full width with double-line or subtle decorative rules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Collapses into a balanced single column or tight 2-column format with compressed gutters (`1rem`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** A single-column vertical reading stream with sticky bottom order actions and horizontal category anchor carousels.
- **Menu Leaders:** Menu rows deploy responsive flex layouts. Dish titles align left, dot-leaders stretch horizontally using repeating radial-dot backgrounds, and prices fix to the right edge. When multiple sizes exist, column headers align directly above right-justified price columns.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system refrains from digital neomorphism and heavy glassmorphism, choosing instead a grounded, paper-like tactile depth:

- **Tonal Layers:** The dominant depth model. Surfaces sit naturally on top of the `#FAF7F2` linen background using clean `#FFFFFF` containers bounded by hairline `#E7E2D8` borders.
- **Ambient Hearth Shadows:** For floating sticky navigation, item customization sheets, and active ordering drawers, shadows use warm, diffused umber tones rather than cold grey:
  - `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)` for hovering cards.
  - `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12)` for modal sheets and basket drawers.
- **Tactile Inset:** Selected modifier pills and counter fields utilize an inset border or subtle background tint (`#F5EFEB`) to emulate indented debossing on pressed menu stock.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, refined borders (`roundedness: 1` — 0.25rem / 4px base radius) across primary structural containers, avoiding overtly rounded digital aesthetics to keep an authentic printed feel.

- **Base Cards & Dividers:** `0.25rem` corner radius, emphasizing neat, crisp edges reminiscent of cut Italian paper stock.
- **Chips, Badges & Interactive Tags:** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem) to signify touchability without transitioning into full oval capsules.
- **Primary Buttons:** Subtle `0.375rem` corners, balancing modern touch standards with timeless hospitality framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid terracotta (`#B93826`) background, crisp white typography, `0.375rem` radius, hover transition to deep crimson (`#9E2F20`). Used for "Add to Order", "Reserve Table", and checkout steps.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill with a `1.5px` border in roasted espresso (`#1C1917`) or terracotta, transitioning to a light linen wash (`#F4EFE6`) on hover.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Plain roasted espresso text with an animated bottom underline, utilized for "View Ingredients" and auxiliary actions.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Compact tags with `0.5rem` radius.
- **Vegetarian/Vegan:** Olive tint background (`#F0F3EC`) with `#5C6B47` text.
- **Chef's Special / Wood-Fired:** Warm toasted crust background (`#FAF2DE`) with `#B8821B` text.
- **Spice Level:** Subtle terracotta dot indicator with body text.

### Menu Item Rows & Cards
- **Classic Editorial Row:** Item name in Playfair Display bold, followed by a dotted leader connecting to the price in Plus Jakarta Sans. Ingredients follow on the next line in muted charcoal body text (`#5A5551`).
- **Interactive Multi-Size Cards:** For pizzas offered in 12" and 16" sizes, price columns sit stacked on the right edge with segmented size selectors for seamless ordering.
- **Dish Images:** Contained within soft rectangular frames with warm tone-matched borders (`#E7E2D8`), avoiding borderless edge bleed.

### Form Inputs & Modifiers
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-hewn square and circular elements with terracotta fill and crisp white checks when selected.
- **Crust & Topping Pickers:** Accordion lists on linen surfaces with subtle bottom dividers. Each line item features a quantity stepper (`-`, `1`, `+`) styled in muted stone colors.
- **Text Inputs:** Outlined in `#E7E2D8` with deep charcoal text and focus rings in `#B93826`.

### Order Rail / Floating Order Tray
- A persistent, streamlined bottom bar on mobile and right sidebar on desktop, finished in `#FFFFFF` with ambient hearth shadow, showing order quantity, running total, and a direct "Complete Order" trigger.
